Tests 1–4 are read-only on our own ledger and logs. Test 5 is a separate read-only WebSocket connection, and test 6 is a modify trial; the operator approved both. No bot change was made.

## Test 2 — What would slower REST re-checks cost? (MEASURED)

- **Missed fills:** REST noticed the 22 WS-missed fills after a median **5.1 s**, at most 11.2 s.
- **All fills:** trade → bot knew in a median **0.10 s**, p90 2.4 s, p99 5.9 s.
- **Effect of a slower cadence:** moving the with-position proof from 1 s to 3 s would add at most about 2 s, and only for the roughly 1% of fills the WS misses. That is small next to the 900 s inventory limit and the 300 s pre-kickoff exit.
- **Not measured:** the share of time the 1 s cadence is active. Unresolved intents after each POST also trigger it.

## Test 3 — Balance timing and the cost of account-read failures (MEASURED, code read)

**How it works:**
- Every 5 s the bot takes an equity bracket: balance, positions, balance, then cash pages (`_refresh_periodic_balance`).
- Decisions refresh it once it is 10 s old (15 s maximum age minus a 5 s margin).
- If it is older than 15 s, `SessionRiskGuard.blockers` stops new risk and the planner cancels resting entries.
- Positions are read both here and in the proof trio.

**Sep 24–26:**
- **1,160 entry cancels** with `side_no_longer_desired:account_new_risk_paused`.
- `[risk-diagnostic] refresh_failed` by stage: equity snapshot 203, cash history 126, final balance 88.
- Of those failures, 266 were HTTP 429, 155 unknown and 114 local deferral.

**Reading:**
- Account-endpoint throttling (balance 175, cash 131, history 116, positions 77 429s) directly costs resting orders, and every re-place spends more calls.
- A slower balance cadence alone cuts 429s but leaves fewer retries before the 15 s limit. Cutting duplicate reads (positions twice) and cash-page reads is safer than simply slowing down.

## Test 6 — Modify instead of cancel+create

Not run. A modify from outside the bot would be an unowned order change, and the bot would stop itself (as with the manual closes at 20:24Z). Placing or changing a live order directly is also not something the assistant does.

**Proposed test path:**
1. Build modify into the bot's own executor behind a default-off switch, with ledger intents and ownership preserved.
2. Trial it on reprices after operator approval and a Command 1.
3. Measure outcomes via the order stream.

## Conclusions so far

- **The WS is fast and about 99% complete, but not complete enough to drop REST.**
- The expensive part is the **account-endpoint reads**, which Polymarket throttles near our current load. Throttling cancels about 390 orders a day through risk pauses.

**Supported, low-risk changes:**
1. Remove the duplicate positions read.
2. Slow the with-position proof cadence (1 s → 3 s).
3. Reduce the cash-page reads in the equity bracket.
4. Add per-reason counters for order lookups, then optimize them.
5. Fix the silent `[api-429]` logging.

**Not supported yet:** raising our 4/s cap; a WS-only balance (pending the test 5 result); modify (pending the in-bot trial).
